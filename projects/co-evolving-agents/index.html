The problem

Easy failures provide limited guidance.

An agent’s own failures do not always reveal the subtle mistakes that separate a promising attempt from success.

+2.1Online shoppingWebShop

Our idea

Learn to generate
more informative failures.

  • Failure agent: Learns from both agents’ failures to generate higher-reward negatives.
  • Target agent: Learns from these hard negatives and provides fresh failures for the failure agent.

Main results

Largest gains on long-horizon reasoning.

All three models improve across every task. The largest gains are on ScienceWorld’s long-horizon scientific reasoning tasks.

Additional sampling

Learned negatives vs.
additional sampling

Both baselines sample four trajectories per instruction.

  • SRLM: builds pairs using self-assigned scores.
  • Best-of-N: selects the highest environment reward.

Learned hard negatives yield higher average reward than either sampling strategy for both models.

Component ablations

Why a failure agent?
Why co-evolution?

  • Positive agent: an extra agent trained toward success.
  • Frozen failure agent: no further updates after iteration 1.

Ours exceeds both variants. The results support learning to generate failures and updating them as the target improves.
